\section{Profile}
PhD researcher at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC) and the University of Barcelona (UB), specialised in large language models (LLMs) as a research method for the social sciences. I use LLMs to measure political content at scale, validate their output against human coding, and build experiments in which participants interact with LLM-driven agents. I apply these methods to online information diets, political polarization and incivility, combining passive web-tracking data with surveys of the same panelists.
